PERSONAL OVERVIEW

Mohammad Ali Sakib.

CSE Undergraduate · PUSTAI & Computer Vision

I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Pabna University of Science and Technology (PUST), Bangladesh, where I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science degree. My academic interests include machine learning, computer vision, hyperspectral image processing, image reconstruction, and spectroscopy.

My research contributions apply deep-learning methods to aerial gait recognition, explainable deepfake-video detection, intent detection in undergraduate CSE questions, ultrasound breast-cancer classification, and rice-leaf disease detection. Alongside my research, I have developed mobile applications and web-based projects focused on practical needs.

I aim to strengthen my research and engineering skills through careful experimentation, collaboration, and continuous learning.
